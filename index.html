About

AMSC 2026: Workshop on Applied Mathematics and Scientific Computing is a one-day hybrid workshop held in Kanazawa, Japan, bringing together researchers from Czech Technical University in Prague, Kanazawa University, and partner institutions. The workshop provides a forum for the presentation and discussion of recent advances in applied mathematics and scientific computing, featuring invited talks by researchers and students. It aims to promote the exchange of ideas, strengthen international collaboration, and foster future research initiatives across a broad range of topics in applied mathematics and computational science.

Links to previous meetings can be found here: AMSC1 and AMSC2.
